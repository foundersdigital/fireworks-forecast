Garden fireworks

Back-garden fireworks aren't listed anywhere. They peak on Bonfire Night, the weekend around it, Diwali (Sunday 8 November) and New Year's Eve. By law they must stop at 11pm, except midnight on Bonfire Night and 1am on Diwali and New Year's Eve.
