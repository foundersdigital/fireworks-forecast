New Year's Eve: Thursday 31 December

Displays listed within 10 miles:

  • LEGOLAND Windsor New Year's Eve fireworks (resort guests only), Windsor (8.9 miles), fireworks 21:30. Only guests staying overnight at the resort can watch, but the bangs will carry to nearby homes in Windsor, Dedworth, Winkfield and Ascot, so plan the evening walk and settle pets before 9:30pm.
  • Garden fireworks are allowed until 1am. Expect the loudest stretch around midnight, with scattered bangs through the evening.
  • Walk the dog in daylight. Sunset is around 4pm.
  • Curtains closed and the radio on by 11:30pm, and stay with your pet through midnight. The London fireworks on TV are loud too.
  • Keep cats in from dusk, with a litter tray indoors.

Garden fireworks

Back-garden fireworks aren't listed anywhere. They peak around Bonfire Night, Diwali and New Year's Eve. By law they must stop at 11pm, except midnight on Bonfire Night and 1am on New Year's Eve, Diwali and Chinese New Year.
