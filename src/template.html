The big nights for garden fireworks

Back-garden fireworks aren't listed anywhere. These are the nights they peak. By law, fireworks must stop at 11pm, except on the nights shown with a later time.

Before the bangs

  • Walk earlyDo the evening walk before dusk and keep them on the lead. Frightened dogs bolt.
  • Make a denA covered crate or quiet corner with their own bedding gives them somewhere to hide.
  • Shut out the nightClose windows and curtains, and put the TV or radio on to soften the bangs.
  • Keep cats inBring them in before dark, close the cat flap and put out a litter tray.
  • Comfort them if they come to youIt's fine to reassure a scared pet. If they'd rather hide, let them.
  • Very scared? Call your vet nowThere's still time to get help in place before New Year's Eve.
